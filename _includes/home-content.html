Evolutionary genomics

We are a multidisciplinary research group based in the Smurfit Institute of Genetics at Trinity College Dublin.

We study how genomes evolve, with a particular focus on the origins and consequences of ploidy change in cancer, the developmental history of tumours, and evolutionary variation across genomes.

Current questions

What processes determine the spectrum of ploidy changes observed across human cancers, and how do these changes influence tumour evolution and clinical outcome?

What can the genomes of testicular germ cell and trophoblastic tumours reveal about the relationship between development and cancer?

We are also interested in the evolution of endogenous viral elements and, separately, the evolutionary history of histone mRNA processing across eukaryotes.
